DiskHealth 1.0.6

Disk health,at a glance.

Check each drive’s health from the Mac menu bar, or keep the desktop widget in view to follow CPU, memory and swap usage.

Download for MacView source

macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon

What your drive reports

DiskHealth reads SMART data through macOS interfaces. Open a drive card to inspect the measurements your device exposes.

NVMe
Controller data such as temperature, available spare, estimated endurance used and media errors.
ATA / SATA
The drive’s reported SMART status and available counts for reallocated or pending sectors.
Unknown
Some USB enclosures do not pass SMART data through. DiskHealth marks the drive as unknown when it has too little information to assess it.

Two independent refresh controls

Drives and volumes

Read once at startup, then refresh manually or choose an interval of 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes or 1 hour.

CPU, memory and swap

Sample system-wide usage manually or every 2 seconds. Pausing keeps the most recent values and their sample time in view.
